# Cannonball – Coast to Coast: approved implementation scope

The user approved the researched 70s-era design and requested implementation on 2026-10-26.
The first deliverable is a playable Pennsylvania vertical slice, not thirteen finished states.

- Native 640×360, 960×540 and 1280×720 selectable without changing simulation.
- Live arcade/cockpit toggle, genuine rear-world mirror, optional paper route map.
- Pennsylvania 10–15 minutes in normal clean driving; at least three counties in every future state.
- Six seeded counties in the sample. Each determines zero or one radar/patrol event per run; no rerolls on reload or on choosing a detour.
- Crossing the Ohio border escapes pursuit. Three confirmed violations fail first, even at the border.
- Mandatory manual shifting for the 930; historical 450 SEL automatic. Two shift buttons, automatic clutch.
- Fuel, mechanical condition/tires, two fatigue values, role-sensitive crew traits, 70s traffic and rivals.
- Save only at safe service stops; saving itself does not heal. All menu real time is frozen. Explicit service actions charge fixed virtual time. Fuel < garage < diner time cost; diner has greater rest benefit. Truckstop combines services.
- All twelve male/female crew definitions and ten vehicle/family definitions; two fully playable sample cars. Family unlock requires a complete Coast-to-Coast campaign, never this single-state prototype.
- Shared route includes NY NJ PA OH IN IL MO KS OK TX NM AZ CA; unfinished states remain visibly unavailable.
- Distinguish historical PRDA fuel-carrying van from Travco motorhome. Expanded tanks are game balancing, not asserted stock capacities.
- New game/save identity, no Velvet Cruise account, badge or save reuse.

Defaults: 360 km virtual Pennsylvania, simulation clock ×8; Porsche theoretical minimum 10.59 minutes at 255 km/h. Six counties, one main/detour branch with actual extra distance, five service stops. Prototype timing may require subsequent human tuning.
