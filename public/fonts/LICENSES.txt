LOCAL FONT BUNDLE

Retrieved 2026-10-26. The WOFF2 files are the unmodified Latin resources returned by Google Fonts.
Only the UI font styles are included: Barlow Condensed normal 400/500/600/700/800/900,
DM Sans normal variable (CSS uses 400-700), and Libre Baskerville italic 400.

The Google Fonts Latin unicode range includes U+0000-00FF, covering German umlauts and sharp s.
The fonts are redistributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; full upstream notices and
copyright statements accompany this bundle in the three OFL-*.txt files.
